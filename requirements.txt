Executive Summary
The meeting focused on defining the business problem, stakeholders, data sources, approach, and next steps for a Head & Neck  project. The primary goal is to develop a method to distinguish Head & Neck versus Lung indications for Rybrevant sales when both indications share the same NDC/SKU, making differentiation impossible from sales data alone. The team agreed to begin with a proof of concept, focusing on data acquisition and experimentation before considering production deployment.
Business Problem
When Benjamin Schriver's team receives Rybrevant sales data (DDD/IntegraChain), they can see that a sale occurred, but they cannot determine whether that sale was for a Head & Neck patient or a Lung cancer patient, because both indications use the same SKU and NDC.

What they know	What they don't know
Account A ordered 100 units of Rybrevant	How much was used for Head & Neck?
Account B ordered 50 units of Rybrevant	How much was used for Lung?

The sales data only says:
Rybrevant sale occurred


It does not say:
Rybrevant sale occurred
→ Head & Neck
or
→ Lung


Why Does the Business Care?
According to Benjamin Schriver, the SIA team needs this information for:
	• Financial forecasting
	• Market share reporting
	• Commercial planning
	• Resource deployment
	• Business performance tracking
	Without indication attribution, they cannot accurately answer questions such as:
		○ How much Head & Neck revenue are we generating?
		○ How much Lung revenue are we generating?
		○ Are Head & Neck sales increasing after launch?
		○ Are forecasts accurate by indication?



Data Sources Discussed
1. Sales Data (DDD / IntegraChain)
	• Daily sales feeds
	• Available at account (L1) level
	• Contains product sales information but no diagnosis labels
	• Primary dataset that requires classification 
2. Claims Data
	• Contains diagnosis information
	• Available at physician (L0/HCP) level
	• Can provide indication labels such as Head & Neck or Lung
	• Has latency and incomplete capture limitations 
3. OPAL Affiliation Data
	• Maps HCPs to accounts
	• Enables linking claims data with sales data
	• Acts as the bridge between labeled claims activity and sales orders 
4. Lab Data
	• Available primarily for lung patients
Could provide strong negative examples and additional signal for model development
